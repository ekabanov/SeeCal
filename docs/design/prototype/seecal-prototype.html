Interactive prototype · iOS 26

SeeCal

Photograph a meal, scan a package barcode, or enter nutrition manually. Meal photos stay on this iPhone; barcode lookup contacts Open Food Facts.

The wait is designed, not hiddenInference takes 15–30 s on device. The analyzing screen narrates real pipeline stages and surfaces the depth measurement early, so waiting feels like work being done.
Capture is coachedPortion accuracy depends on a 35–45 cm top-down shot. Distance and level are shown live; the shutter is never blocked — guidance, not gating.
One camera, three entry pathsScan still opens the camera immediately. The same view detects package barcodes automatically and keeps manual entry one tap away without slowing photo capture.
Output is a draftMeal names, ingredients, amounts, calories and macros are editable. Corrected nutrition becomes the new density and continues to scale with grams.
Totals stay trustworthyMeal totals always equal the sum of its ingredients. Items can be added, removed with Undo, or reset to the model estimate.
